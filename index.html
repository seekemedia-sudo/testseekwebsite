Frequently asked questions

How much does a car repair estimate cost?

Estimates are free and no-obligation. We inspect the vehicle, send photos of what we find, and give you a written price before any work begins.

How long will my repair take?

Most oil changes, brake jobs, batteries and diagnostics finish the same day, usually in one to three hours. Larger engine and transmission work typically takes two to four days, and we confirm the timeline before we start.

Do you offer a warranty on repairs?

Yes. Qualifying repairs are backed by a 24-month / 24,000-mile written warranty on parts and labor, honored at our shop and through our nationwide network.

Do you work on my make and model?

We service domestic, Asian and European vehicles, including hybrids and EVs. If a repair truly requires a dealer-only tool, we tell you up front instead of experimenting on your car.

Can I bring my own parts?

In most cases yes, though customer-supplied parts cannot be covered by our warranty. We will always quote you our parts pricing first so you can compare.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We offer interest-free financing options on qualifying repairs so a major failure does not have to wait.
